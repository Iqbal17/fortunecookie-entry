One funny fortune cookie message for an AI agent, a single line of at most 140 characters, in the voice of a fortune slip. Free to enter, $2 across 10 places, a house judge compares the fortunes two at a time and the funniest, most original ones take the pot.

## Run it

```sh
python3 -P -s main.py
```
